# Requesting website unblock, guest wifi or firewall change

How to request a blocked website to be whitelisted, guest wifi for visitors, or a firewall port. This is a Service Request.

## Steps

1. Provide the URL or port, business reason and the approving manager.
2. Guest wifi: give visitor names and dates; credentials are valid 24 hours.
3. Firewall changes need Change Advisory approval and take 2 working days.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
