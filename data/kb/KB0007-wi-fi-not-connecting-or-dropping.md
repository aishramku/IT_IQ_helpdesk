# Wi-Fi not connecting or dropping

Symptoms: cannot connect to office wifi, limited connectivity, no internet, wifi drops in meeting rooms.

## Steps

1. Toggle Wi-Fi off and on and forget then rejoin the corporate SSID.
2. Restart the laptop and confirm Airplane mode is off.
3. Move closer to an access point; note the floor and room if drops persist.
4. Update the wireless driver from Software Centre.
5. Report widespread drops with floor and time so Network Operations can check the access point.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
