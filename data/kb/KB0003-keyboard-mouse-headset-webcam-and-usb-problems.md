# Keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam and USB problems

Symptoms: keys not working, mouse lagging, headset or webcam not detected in Teams, USB ports dead.

## Steps

1. Replace batteries in wireless devices and re-pair them.
2. Try a different USB port and a different device to isolate the fault.
3. In Teams open Settings > Devices and select the correct microphone, speaker and camera.
4. Restart the laptop; Windows will reinstall the driver.
5. Faulty peripherals are replaced from stock by Desktop Support.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
