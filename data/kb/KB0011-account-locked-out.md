# Account locked out

Symptoms: account locked after wrong attempts, cannot sign in, account has been locked.

## Steps

1. Wait 15 minutes; accounts unlock automatically.
2. Use the self-service portal Unlock account option with MFA.
3. Remove old saved passwords from your phone mail app, a common cause of repeat lockouts.
4. If still locked, Identity & Access verifies you before unlocking.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
