# Mailbox full, shared mailbox and distribution lists

Symptoms: mailbox full, storage quota exceeded, cannot send. Also requests to be added to a distribution list, shared mailbox or calendar permission.

## Steps

1. Empty Deleted Items and enable the Online Archive (File > Info > Tools).
2. Archive large attachments with Sort by Size.
3. Distribution lists, shared mailboxes and calendar delegation are Service Requests approved by the owner.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
