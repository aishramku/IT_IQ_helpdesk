# Windows update stuck or failing

Symptoms: update stuck at a percentage, restart loop after update, error 0x80070057, 0x800f0922, pending restart.

## Steps

1. Restart the laptop and let the update continue for 30 minutes on power.
2. Run the Windows Update troubleshooter.
3. Free at least 10 GB of disk space.
4. If it keeps failing, Application Support will reset update components or roll back the patch.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
