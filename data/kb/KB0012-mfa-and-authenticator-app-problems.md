# MFA and Authenticator app problems

Symptoms: MFA prompt not arriving, new phone, authenticator has no accounts, verification code loop, AADSTS50076.

## Steps

1. Check phone time is set to automatic and notifications are enabled.
2. Choose Sign in another way and use a backup method or SMS.
3. New phone: raise a ticket to reset MFA; identity is verified through your manager before re-registration.
4. Never share verification codes with anyone.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
