# Requesting new equipment (laptop, monitor, headset, dock)

How to request a new laptop, additional monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset or equipment for a new joiner. This is a Service Request.

## Steps

1. Raise a ticket with the item, business reason and manager name.
2. Manager approval is required; attach the approval email.
3. Standard items ship from stock in 2-3 working days; non-standard items go through procurement.
4. New joiner kits must be requested 5 working days before the start date.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
