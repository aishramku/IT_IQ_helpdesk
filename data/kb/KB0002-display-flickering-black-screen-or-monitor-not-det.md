# Display flickering, black screen or monitor not detected

Symptoms: screen flickers, vertical lines, black screen, external monitor or docking station shows no signal, cracked screen.

## Steps

1. Reseat the HDMI/DisplayPort cable at both ends and try a different port.
2. Press Win+P and choose Extend or Duplicate.
3. Restart the laptop with the dock connected; unplug the dock power for 30 seconds first.
4. Update the display driver from Software Centre.
5. A cracked panel needs a warranty replacement - raise it with Desktop Support and attach a photo.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
