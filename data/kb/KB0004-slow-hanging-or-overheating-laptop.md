# Slow, hanging or overheating laptop

Symptoms: laptop extremely slow, freezes, loud fan, gets hot and shuts down, disk at 100 percent.

## Steps

1. Restart at least once a week; do not just close the lid.
2. Open Task Manager and end heavy start-up apps; check Disk and Memory columns.
3. Run Disk Cleanup and uninstall unused software.
4. Keep vents clear; use the laptop on a hard surface.
5. If it stays slow, Desktop Support can check RAM, thermal paste and disk health.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
