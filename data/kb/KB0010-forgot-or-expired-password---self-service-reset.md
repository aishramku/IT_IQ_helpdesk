# Forgot or expired password - self-service reset

Symptoms: forgot password, password expired, new password not accepted, outlook keeps asking for password.

## Steps

1. Go to the self-service password portal and choose Forgot password.
2. Verify with MFA on your registered phone and set a new password of at least 12 characters.
3. After changing it, update the password on your phone mail app and sign out of Outlook once.
4. Agents NEVER send passwords by email or chat and never reset without identity verification.
5. If self-service fails, raise a ticket; Identity & Access will verify you via the callback number on file.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
