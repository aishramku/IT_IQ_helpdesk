# Outlook not connecting, cannot send or receive email

Symptoms: Outlook shows disconnected, trying to connect, emails stuck in outbox, mails bouncing, error 0x800CCC0E.

## Steps

1. Check the IT status page for a known mail outage before troubleshooting.
2. Make sure Work Offline is off (Send/Receive tab).
3. Close Outlook and start it with outlook /safe.
4. Create a new Outlook profile via Control Panel > Mail.
5. If many colleagues have the same problem, do not keep raising tickets - the Messaging team will post updates on the incident.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
