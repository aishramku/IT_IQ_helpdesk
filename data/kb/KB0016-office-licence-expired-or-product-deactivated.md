# Office licence expired or product deactivated

Symptoms: Microsoft Office says product deactivated, unlicensed product banner, read-only mode, error 0xC004F074.

## Steps

1. Sign out of all Office apps, then sign in again with your work account.
2. Open an Office app > File > Account > Update Options > Update Now.
3. If the banner persists, Application Support will reassign your licence.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
