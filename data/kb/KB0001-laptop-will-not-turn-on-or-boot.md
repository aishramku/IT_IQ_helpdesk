# Laptop will not turn on or boot

Symptoms: laptop or desktop does not power on, stuck on the logo screen, blue screen, boot loop, error 0xc000021a / INACCESSIBLE_BOOT_DEVICE.

## Steps

1. Disconnect all peripherals and docks, then hold the power button for 30 seconds.
2. Plug in the original charger and wait 15 minutes; check the charging light.
3. If it reaches the logo and restarts, power off, then press F8/F11 to open Windows Recovery and choose Startup Repair.
4. Note the stop code shown on the blue screen and add it to the ticket.
5. If nothing works Desktop Support will issue a loaner laptop and arrange hardware diagnostics.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
