# Application crashes, freezes or not responding

Symptoms: Excel, SAP, Teams, Salesforce or another app keeps crashing, not responding, will not open after an update.

## Steps

1. Close the app fully in Task Manager and reopen.
2. For Office apps use Settings > Apps > Modify > Quick Repair.
3. Start in safe mode and disable add-ins (Excel: hold Ctrl while opening).
4. Clear the app cache (Teams: delete the Teams folder in AppData).
5. Send the exact error code and time to Application Support if it recurs.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
