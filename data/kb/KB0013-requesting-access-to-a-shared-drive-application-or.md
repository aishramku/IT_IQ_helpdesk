# Requesting access to a shared drive, application or role

How to request access to a shared folder, SharePoint site, SAP role, Jira project or CRM. This is a Service Request needing approval.

## Steps

1. Name the resource, the access level and the business reason.
2. The data or application owner must approve; managers can approve standard roles.
3. Access is granted by Identity & Access once approval is attached - agents cannot grant it automatically.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
