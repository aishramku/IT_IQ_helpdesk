# Printer offline, paper jam, stuck queue or adding a printer

Symptoms: printer offline, paper jam, error 79, print job stuck, blank or streaky prints, toner low. Also adding a printer or scan-to-email.

## Steps

1. Check the printer display and power; clear any paper jam following the on-screen diagram.
2. Cancel stuck jobs: Settings > Printers > Open queue > Cancel all.
3. Restart the print spooler service or the printer, then print a test page.
4. Add a printer: Settings > Printers > Add and select it by the label on the device (e.g. PRN-OMR-2F-03).
5. If several users in one office cannot print, report the floor and printer label so Desktop Support can check the device and print server.

## Escalation

If the steps above do not fix it, reply to your ticket with what you tried.
